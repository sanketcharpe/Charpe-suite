CHARPE SUITE — OFFICIAL eCOURTS: ONE-TIME SETUP (about 15 minutes)
====================================================================

PART A — Put the eCourts backend in your Google account
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Open https://script.google.com on your laptop and click "New project" (top left).
2. Click on "Untitled project" at the top and rename it:  Charpe eCourts
3. Delete everything in the editor (Ctrl+A, then Delete).
4. Open the file  eCourtsOfficial.gs  from this zip in Notepad, press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C,
   and paste it into the editor (Ctrl+V).
5. Near the top, fill in the two lines:
     var SECRET    = 'PUT-YOUR-SYNC-PASSWORD-HERE';
       → replace the text between the quotes with a password of your choice
         (you may use the same password as your Drive sync).
     var FOLDER_ID = 'PUT-YOUR-DRIVE-FOLDER-ID-HERE';
       → open your "Charpe Suite Data" folder in Google Drive, copy the long code
         after /folders/ in the address bar, and paste it between the quotes.
6. Press Ctrl+S to save.
7. In the toolbar, pick the function "authorise" from the drop-down next to "Debug",
   then click "Run". Google asks for permission:
     Review permissions → choose your account → "Advanced" → "Go to Charpe eCourts (unsafe)"
     → Allow.   (It says "unsafe" only because you wrote the script yourself.)
8. Click the blue "Deploy" button (top right) → "New deployment".
     - Click the gear icon next to "Select type" → choose "Web app".
     - Description: eCourts
     - Execute as: Me
     - Who has access: Anyone
     - Click "Deploy".
9. Copy the "Web app URL" it shows (it ends in /exec). Keep it handy.

PART B — Put the new files on your GitHub site
----------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://github.com/sanketcharpe/Charpe-suite
2. Click "Add file" → "Upload files".
3. From this zip, drag in:
     cases.html
     sw.js
     the "assets" folder (it contains ecourts.js)
     the "apps-script" folder (keeps a copy of the backend with your site)
4. At the bottom, click "Commit changes". Wait one or two minutes.

PART C — Connect the app
------------------------
1. Open your Charpe Suite, sign in to Cases.
2. Settings → "Official eCourts".
3. Paste the Web app URL from Part A step 9 and the password from Part A step 5.
   Choose which cases to offer (default: next date today or earlier). Click "Save".
4. Click "Test connection". You should see two green ticks:
     ✓ Backend answers   ✓ eCourts site reachable
5. On the master desktop (Chrome/Edge): click "Choose folder" and pick a folder such as
   Documents\Charpe Orders. Every order will also be saved there as a PDF.
   Do the same on the second desktop if you want copies there; then click
   "Copy all saved orders to this PC" once.
   These settings reach your other computers and phone through Drive sync automatically
   (except the PC folder, which is chosen on each computer).

DAILY USE
---------
• Click "⚖ eCourts" at the top. Today's and overdue cases are already ticked.
• Click "Show CAPTCHAs", type each one (Enter jumps to the next), then "Fetch all"
  — or "Fetch" on a single row.
• Orders download in the background. Each case shows "Orders (n)" — click Open.
• Orders open from this browser first, then the PC folder, then Drive. No CAPTCHA.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
• If a case shows "eCourts has no case for this CNR" or comes back with no dates,
  the backend saves what eCourts sent as  _ecourts_last_response.html  in your
  Charpe Suite Data folder. Send that file to Claude and the reading can be adjusted.
• After changing the script later: Deploy → Manage deployments → pencil icon →
  Version: "New version" → Deploy. The URL stays the same.
