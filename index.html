            Upload the daily board

            Choose the board (cause list) as a PDF — or paste its text. The tool reads each entry's serial number, case number, parties and stage, finds your matters in the Library by case number, and pins them to that day's list in board order. Check the list, then press Pin.

            Sync with Google Drive and the iPad
            1. Keep matters in this browser (not in a PC folder) on every device. Matters in a PC folder do not sync; use Move to synced storage on each.
            2. Open Settings, paste the sync address and key, and press Sync now. Do the same on the iPad.
            3. From then on notes, highlights, drawings, sheets and PDFs sync by themselves. The ☁ button shows when it last synced.

              Problem report

              If the app misbehaves or reloads by itself, press Copy problem report and paste it to Claude. It lists recent actions and device details, not the contents of your matters.

              Google Drive sync

              For the iPad, or any device that keeps matters on the device itself: matters are stored here and synced with the Excerpt Desk folder in your Google Drive, the same folder your laptop uses. Paste the two values from your Apps Script setup.

              Backup on this device

              Clear the worksheet?

              You can still bring it back with Undo while this matter stays open, and a copy is saved in the matter's Backups folder where possible. The PDF files themselves are never changed.
