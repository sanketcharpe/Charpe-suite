CHARPE HELPER  (Oct 2026)

Why: the Supreme Court site turns away Google's servers, and the MAT site
cannot be reached from them. Charpe Helper runs quietly on this PC and fetches
those two sites for Charpe Suite from this office's own internet connection.
CAT, eCourts and Google Drive keep working through the eCourts backend as before.

Install (once on each PC that fetches cases):
  1. Right-click Charpe-Helper.zip > Extract All > Extract.
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Install Charpe Helper.bat".
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click More info > Run anyway.
  4. A browser tab opens saying "Charpe helper is running". Close it.
  5. In Charpe Suite: Sync Centre > Supreme Court · CAT · MAT should show
     "Charpe Helper is running on this PC". If Chrome asks to allow
     sanketcharpe.github.io to access devices on your local network, click Allow.

It starts by itself with Windows. To check it at any time, open
http://127.0.0.1:8770/ in the browser.

It listens only on this computer and answers only Charpe Suite's web address.
To remove it: double-click "Remove Charpe Helper.bat".
